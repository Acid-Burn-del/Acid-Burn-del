## Context

Omendon is an AI-powered go-to-market-as-a-service platform based in Abu Dhabi
and Dubai. It helps companies, especially from the Nordics, enter new markets
faster and with less risk. The brand had to feel credible to government partners
and investors while still reading as a modern tech product.

## Approach

- **Globe mark:** flowing lines that break into dots, showing expansion moving
  outward from a solid core into new markets.
- **One strong colour:** a single teal gives the brand one recognisable colour in
  a category full of navy and gold, with slate grey for the enterprise side.
- **Typography:** Poppins keeps everything geometric and easy to read for
  international audiences.

## The system

A 20-page brand guideline covering the logo mark, wordmark, lockups, clear space,
minimum size, colour, typography scale, tone of voice and brand values, applied
across stationery, merchandise, event materials, the website and every social
profile.
