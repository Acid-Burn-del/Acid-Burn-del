## Context

Final group project for the Data Processing with Python course at HAMK. We
analysed 1,000 bookings from a city hotel in Lisbon and a resort hotel in the
Algarve (arrivals July 2015 – August 2017), using the published
[hotel booking demand dataset](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126).

## My part

- **Research question 1:** cross-tabulations comparing cancellation behaviour
  between the two hotels.
- **Research question 2:** `groupby` analysis of booking patterns and prices.
- **Conclusions:** pulling the findings together for the report.

## Tools

Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Jupyter notebooks.
