## Context

As a Course Production Assistant at Sense Valmennus Oy, I supported the technical
development and production of e-learning content. The work combined creativity,
technology and design to improve digital learning experiences.

## What I did

- Edited and produced course videos with subtitles, graphics and branding elements.
- Formatted and integrated learning materials (PDFs, guides, exercises) into the LearnWorlds LMS.
- Tested and optimised course flow for a smooth learner experience.
- Developed interactive features such as quizzes, polls and gamified content.
- Designed simple visuals (icons, infographics, cover images) for online courses.
- Worked with the social media team on course teasers and promotional clips.

## What I learned

LMS management, multimedia editing and UX for e-learning, educational technology,
and using analytics to optimise courses.
